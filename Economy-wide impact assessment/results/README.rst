Electricity scenarios for Italy
===============================

These files are generated by the companion
``economy_wide_impact_assessment.ipynb`` using ``aggregation.xlsx``.
They contain Italy's EXIOBASE 3.8.2 monetary SUT for reference year 2022,
with 18 products, 29 activities and twelve individual electricity-generation
technologies. Its electricity supply mix is updated to 2022 with MARIO
and EMBER. Direct electricity-emission coefficients are taken at runtime
from the public EXIOBASE 3.11.2 ixi IOT for 2022. Imports are separate
from domestic factors of production.

Each CSV is a long-format MARIO matrix for ``baseline``, ``High_RES`` and
``High_RES_domestic``. ``sets.xlsx`` contains the matching classifications.
Monetary flow values are in EUR and GHG flows in kg CO2-equivalent, using
IPCC AR6 GWP100. ``scenario_summary.csv`` reports M EUR and kt CO2-equivalent.
The notebook and guide define the scenario assumptions and their limitations.

The source 3.8.2 economic data and classifications retain CC-BY-SA-4.0.
The 3.11.2 emission correction has a separate source licence. Retain the
source attribution and review the corresponding conditions for reuse.

* EXIOBASE 3.8.2 data and licence https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.5589597
* Stadler et al., EXIOBASE 3 https://doi.org/10.1111/jiec.12715
* Licence https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

The nine CSV files include corrected ``Ea`` and ``S`` and omit ``Xa``.
``IMP_c`` and ``VY`` are also omitted because they are zero in all scenarios.
No corrected coefficient tables or filled emission-shock workbooks are included.
The sibling ``cvxlab_model`` folder contains the classified
CVXLab sets, settings, reference input workbook and scenario shocks.

* EXIOBASE 3.11.2 https://zenodo.org/records/23039074
* Emission-correction source licence https://zenodo.org/records/23039074/files/LICENSE.txt?download=1
